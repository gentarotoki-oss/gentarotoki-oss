# Gentaro Toki

Undergraduate in Mathematical Informatics at the University of Tokyo (Dept. of Mathematical Engineering and Information Physics).
Aiming to be a quant trader. Interested in high-frequency trading and market microstructure: market making, limit order books, and short-horizon signals.

## Featured projects

| Project | What it does | Stack |
|---|---|---|
| [market-making-sim](https://github.com/gentarotoki-oss/market-making-sim) | Avellaneda–Stoikov market maker with a reproduction of the paper's experiment, and a price-time priority matching engine (~300 ns/op). | Rust |
| [crypto-cross-venue](https://github.com/gentarotoki-oss/crypto-cross-venue) | Cross-venue study of equity perps (TSLA, MSTR, QQQ) on Binance, Bybit and OKX: gaps close within seconds, Binance leads, and trading the gap does not survive taker fees. | Python, pandas |
| [climate-derivatives](https://github.com/gentarotoki-oss/climate-derivatives) | Prices HDD/CDD futures and options on daily temperature. Seasonal + Ornstein-Uhlenbeck temperature model, Burn Analysis vs. Monte Carlo under a risk-neutral measure with a market price of risk, interactive dashboard. | Python, NumPy, SciPy, FastAPI, React, TypeScript, D3.js, pytest |
| [bert-japanese-sentiment](https://github.com/gentarotoki-oss/bert-japanese-sentiment) | Japanese sentiment classification CLI built on a Japanese BERT model. | Python, Hugging Face Transformers |
